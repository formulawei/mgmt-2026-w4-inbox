## Restricted teaching sources

- Charles Conn and Robert McLean, *Bulletproof Problem Solving*: use conceptual paraphrases, original teaching prompts, and source attribution; do not reproduce chapters, figures, or lengthy passages.
- “找不出根因，做不好決策”: adapt only the minimum facts needed for the chain-store classroom case. Do not include its URL, saved HTML, full text, or original figures in the website or ZIP.
- NTU COOL student questions: synthesize common difficulties; do not expose student names or identifiable comments.

## Classroom-case policy

The activity intentionally adds fictionalized evidence and counterevidence. These additions are labeled as course adaptations and are not attributed to the source article. The article's published causal chain is a teacher reference, not a unique answer key.
